X-Tech Laboratories is just getting started, which makes this the best time to help shape it.

## Bring a project

Maintain a project that competes with one of ours, or that could combine with another? Talk to us
about bringing it under one roof, where its best features reach more people and more people can
maintain it. See [consolidation](/guidelines/consolidation/) for how we go about it.

Built something with AI that you'd like others to use and improve? Before it becomes another
one-person project, consider bringing it into X-Tech Laboratories.

Maintain an open source project that welcomes AI-assisted contributions? Suggest it for
[Awesome AI-Assisted Open Source](/awesome). And before starting something new, check whether an existing project could take your
idea as a plugin or a setting instead.

## Join the discussion

Plugin interfaces, CI recipes, disclosure formats and agent guidelines are open questions, and we want many
voices on them.

- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/x-tech-laboratories/discussions): ideas, questions and longer
  conversations that should stay findable.
- [#ai-floss:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#ai-floss:matrix.org): the main X-Tech Laboratories channel on Matrix.
- [#ai-floss-general:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#ai-floss-general:matrix.org): general chat.

For concrete proposals, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/x-tech-laboratories.github.io/issues).

## Build the tooling

Templates, review bots, CI recipes and documentation all need hands, human and otherwise. Browse
the repositories at [github.com/x-tech-laboratories](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories).
