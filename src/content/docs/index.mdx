## Principles

1. **People stay accountable.** Whoever submits a change owns it, however it was written.
2. **Be open about the tools.** Disclosure is normal and expected, never a mark against a contribution.
3. **Tests before trust.** Code is accepted because it is verified, not because of who or what wrote it.
4. **Readable by people.** All code stays simple enough for a person to review and maintain without AI.
5. **Respect licences and upstreams.** Credit the projects we build on and bring together, and follow their licences.
6. **Lighten the load on maintainers.** Automation and AI should reduce the work of reviewing contributions, not add to it.
7. **Build together, not alone.** Improve a shared project before starting a new one, and build it to last beyond its first user.
8. **Extend, don't fork.** Settle differences with plugins, settings and open formats, so one project can serve many needs.
9. **Share what works.** The guidelines, templates and tooling we build are free software too.
