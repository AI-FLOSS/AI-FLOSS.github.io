A curated list of free and open source software developed with substantial AI assistance, where the
maintainers say so publicly. Every entry links to the evidence.

Last checked October 2026. Each project also shows its [AI Readiness Score](/guidelines/readiness/), measured automatically from its repository.

## Criteria

- **Free software.** Released under an OSI-approved or FSF-recognised free licence.
- **Openly AI-assisted.** The maintainers say in their own sources (a policy, README, release
  notes or routine commit trailers) that AI tools play a real part in its development. Shipping an AI feature, or only having an `AGENTS.md`, is not enough.
- **Alive.** Actively maintained, with commits in the last six months.

## Suggest a project

Open a pull request that adds an entry to
[`src/data/awesome.json`](https://github.com/AI-FLOSS/AI-FLOSS.github.io/blob/main/src/data/awesome.json)
with its name, repository, SPDX licence, category, a one-line description, and a link to the
maintainers' own evidence of AI use. The readiness score is filled in automatically.
