## X-Music

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Music.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A desktop music player with a real library engine.** X-Music brings together gmusicbrowser's data
model and power with a user interface people can re-layout and re-skin, and imports an existing
gmusicbrowser library. A track is a piece of music, not a file: it survives renames, moves, format
changes and retagging, so your ratings and play history never get lost.

X-Music is our flagship project and is in early development. The library core, query engine,
playback and gmusicbrowser import work; tag writing and media keys are still to come.

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Music) · GPL-3.0-or-later ·
[Contribute](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Music/blob/main/AGENTS.md)

## X-Stream

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Stream.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A privacy-respecting YouTube client.** X-Stream lets you search, watch and subscribe to YouTube
channels without being tracked by Google. Your subscriptions and history live on your own device,
and it imports them from FreeTube.

X-Stream is in early development. Search, channels, subscriptions, history and adaptive playback
up to 2160p work in the desktop app; installers, mobile builds and sync between devices are still
to come.

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Stream) · GPL-3.0-or-later ·
[Contribute](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Stream/blob/main/AGENTS.md)

## X-Monitor

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/X-Monitor.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**A network and system monitor for Linux.** X-Monitor watches your machines, homelab and small
network from one NiceGUI dashboard, and can act on what it watches, such as restarting a systemd
service. Hosts you control run an optional agent that streams events as they happen; routers,
switches and appliances are monitored without one, over SSH, HTTP, DNS and ICMP.

X-Monitor was called Vigil. Monitors are plugins, so new ones are small and easy to test, and
results reach you through notifications, Prometheus and InfluxDB.

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Monitor) · GPL-3.0-or-later ·
[Contribute](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/X-Monitor/blob/main/AGENTS.md)

## ai-readiness

[![AI readiness](https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url=https://x-tech-laboratories.github.io/badges/x-tech-laboratories/ai-readiness.json)](/guidelines/readiness/)

**Scores how ready a repository is for AI-assisted development.** A command-line tool, library and
GitHub Action that checks any public repository against the eight
[AI Readiness Score](/guidelines/readiness/) checks and shows the evidence for each.

[Source code](https://github.com/x-tech-laboratories/ai-readiness) · MIT ·
`npx github:x-tech-laboratories/ai-readiness owner/repo`
